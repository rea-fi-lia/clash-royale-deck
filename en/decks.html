How to read this page

Decks can be sorted three ways: Usage, Win Rate, and Rising. Usage is how many people play the deck, Win Rate is how much the deck wins overall, and Rising highlights archetypes gaining players recently. Meta share summarizes how much each win condition is being played.

In the win-rate view, “3-crown rate” is the share of wins that ended in three crowns, and “crown diff” is crowns taken minus crowns lost per match. Two decks with the same win rate can differ here: one steamrolls, the other grinds out chip damage.
